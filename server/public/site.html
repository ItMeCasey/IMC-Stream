WHAT IT IS

Three simple pages you can send viewers to: your commands, your games and how text-to-speech works (with the voice and every sound effect to listen to). They are made from what is switched on in the app right now. There is no server and no domain to pay for: it is a folder you put on a free host such as Cloudflare Pages.

1. BUILD IT

Build it again whenever you change commands, games or TTS, then upload the folder again.

2. PUBLISH ON CLOUDFLARE PAGES (FREE, AUTOMATIC)

Log in to Cloudflare once and the app does the rest: it makes the project, uploads the pages, and uploads again by itself whenever your commands, games or text-to-speech change. You never upload anything by hand.

Checking...
A browser window opens: log in (or make a free account) and press Allow. This app never sees your password.
No Node.js, or would rather do it by hand?
  1. Press Build the website above, then go to dash.cloudflare.com and open Workers & Pages.
  2. Press Create, choose Pages and then Upload assets, name the project, drag the website folder in and press Deploy.
  3. To update it later: Create new deployment and drag the folder in again.

The automatic way needs Node.js on this PC (it uses Cloudflare's own tool, wrangler, which is fetched the first time).
