HOW IT WORKS

A viewer spends channel points on a reward and pastes the link of an emote from 7TV (https://7tv.app/emotes/...). The app adds that emote to your real 7TV emote set, so everyone with 7TV sees it, and takes it away again after the number of days you choose. If the link is wrong, the emote is not allowed, or your set is full, the viewer is told why and the points are refunded.

Only 7TV links work. BetterTTV has no way to add emotes to a channel automatically, so BetterTTV links are refused and refunded. Emotes marked as adult, flashing or not allowed on Twitch are always refused.

1. CONNECT YOUR 7TV ACCOUNT

  1. Open 7tv.app and log in with Twitch (the same account as this channel).
  2. Press F12, open the Application tab (Storage in Firefox), then Local Storage and https://7tv.app.
  3. Copy the value of the entry called token (or 7tv-token) and paste it below. It is a long text that starts with eyJ.

The token is kept on this PC only, in the app's data folder. It is never shown again and never sent anywhere but 7TV. It gives this app the same rights as you on 7TV, so do not share it. If it stops working, log in again and paste a fresh one.
