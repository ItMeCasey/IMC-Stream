HOW PANELS WORK

Panels are the boxes under your stream on your Twitch channel page. Twitch has no way for apps to change them (there is no panel API), so this page can't push them for you. Here you design them, download the images, and the app keeps them in sync with chat commands, so viewers get the same info in chat (!discord, !rules...).

  1. Make a panel on the left, pick its look, press Download image.
  2. Press Open my channel page, switch on Edit Panels, add an Image panel, upload the image and paste the link.
  3. Tick Also answer in chat and press Save + sync to chat.
