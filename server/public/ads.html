RUN AN AD NOW

Starts a commercial immediately (you must be live, and Affiliate or Partner). Twitch limits how often you can run them. Click twice to confirm.

Posts: "Ad break starting now (60s). Thanks for sticking around!"

SNOOZE

Pushes the next automatic ad back about 5 minutes. Twitch gives you a few snoozes that refill over time.

From chat (mods): !ad 60 runs an ad, !snooze snoozes. Everyone: !nextad.

CHAT MESSAGES AROUND ADS

Sent by the chat bot. Pick Announcement for the highlighted, colored banner in chat (the look of Fossabot's "Twitch is rolling a quick ad break" message).

Announcements need the "announcements" permission: press Connect Twitch again once.
