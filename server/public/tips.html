1. YOUR TIP LINK

Your own small tip service on a free Cloudflare account tells this app the moment a tip is paid, so the alert plays within seconds. If this PC is off, the tip alerts when it comes back. You never type a PayPal or Ko-fi password here. Where do you want to take tips?

  1. Make a free Cloudflare account (skip this if you already have one).
  2. Open Cloudflare API Tokens, press Create Token, and next to Edit Cloudflare Workers press Use template. Choose your account if it asks, then Continue to summary and Create Token. Copy the token (Cloudflare shows it only once).
  3. In Ko-fi, open Settings → API and copy the Verification Token from the Webhooks part.
  4. Fill in the boxes below and press Set up my tip link. The app puts the tip service on your Cloudflare account for you; your token is used once and not saved.

3. ALERT FOR A TIP

Tips paid on your tip page alert by themselves. For a tip that arrived another way (cash, a different service), log it here and the on-stream tip alert plays too, and it counts toward your session tip total.

4. CATCH-UP CHECK (OPTIONAL)

The app can check your PayPal account for new payments by itself. PayPal reports payments with a delay (usually minutes, sometimes a few hours), so an alert may come late. It needs a free PayPal developer app that only you can see, and the keys stay on this PC.

  1. Go to developer.paypal.com → Apps & Credentials → Live and press Create App.
  2. Under Features, tick Transaction Search, save.
  3. Copy the Client ID and Secret here.
