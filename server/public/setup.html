1. CONNECT TWITCH

Opens Twitch in your browser: log in and press Authorize. Nothing to copy or paste.
Advanced: use my own Twitch app (Client ID)

Create a free app at dev.twitch.tv/console (Category: Application Integration, Client Type: Public), copy its Client ID here, then press Connect.

2. OBS SCENES

Installs the ItMeCasey scene collection (Starting Soon, Gameplay, Just Chatting, Full Cam, Be Right Back, Ending) wired to this app. Close OBS first, or use Download and import it in OBS > Scene Collection > Import.

OBS WEBSOCKET

Needed for scene switching and reward hooks. In OBS: Tools > WebSocket Server Settings > enable.

AUDIO + VOD (VOICEMEETER)

Keeps your music and alert sounds out of the Twitch VOD (so you don't get muted sections) while viewers still hear everything live. Voicemeeter splits the sounds, and OBS leaves the music and alerts off audio Track 2, the track Twitch saves for the VOD. Banana is enough for this; Potato just has more inputs.

1. GET VOICEMEETER

The files come only from VB-Audio's own site, and the app checks they are signed by VB-Audio before running them. Windows will ask permission, then Voicemeeter's installer opens: press Install, then restart Windows (it installs audio drivers).

2. WINDOWS SETTINGS (about 1 minute)

  1. Sound settings → Output: set your default output to Voicemeeter Input (VB-Audio Voicemeeter VAIO). Games, browser and system sounds then go through Voicemeeter.
  2. Sound settings → Input: leave your real microphone as the default (Voicemeeter reads it directly).
  3. Volume mixer / App volume: for your music player (Spotify, browser, ...), set the output to Voicemeeter Aux Input. That is what keeps music off the VOD. Discord can stay on the main one (or use Aux too if you don't want chat on stream).

3. SET UP VOICEMEETER

This routes your mic and game/chat sound to B1 (stream + VOD) and your music (Aux input) to B2 (stream only). You hear everything in your headphones.

4. OBS: NO MUSIC OR ALERTS IN THE VOD

OBS must be running with WebSocket on (step 2 above). Scan, tick the sources that should not be in the VOD (alerts, music players, overlays with sound), then apply. This adds two Voicemeeter inputs, puts them on the right tracks, and mutes the old Desktop Audio and Mic so nothing is doubled.

Two things OBS won't let an app switch, do them once: (a) Settings → Stream: tick Twitch VOD Track Audio (uses Track 2); (b) restart OBS so Advanced output mode takes effect. Then record a short test (Settings → Output → Recording: untick Track 2) or check your next VOD.

BACKUP & RESTORE

Your alerts, commands, hooks, goals, board, uploads and settings. A copy is also saved automatically every day (last 7 kept).

CONTROL FROM ANOTHER DEVICE

Lets your phone or a second PC on your home network open the dashboard and the Board. It always asks for this password. Leave it off if you don't need it.

REALISTIC VOICES (OPTIONAL)

Natural-sounding text-to-speech voices that run on your PC (about 120 MB). Needs Python 3.9–3.13 installed. Without it the normal Windows voices are used.
